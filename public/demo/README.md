# QuikSol demo media

These assets are optimized local copies of conventional stock photographs used only by
the fictional QuikSol commercial demo. They are not photographs of QuikSol employees,
customers, public figures, or the fictional companies named in the demo. The depicted
people and photographers do not endorse QuikSol.

- Employee portraits: 28 WebP files at 512 x 512 in `people/`.
- Company photographs: 19 WebP files at 1200 x 700 in `companies/`.
- Exact source page, original image URL, photographer credit, license URL, dimensions,
  review date, and SHA-256 for every file: `scripts/demo-media-manifest.ts`.
- Source licenses: [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/) and
  [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license).

All selected source pages were reviewed as stock photography and had no AI-generated
disclosure. The downloaded pixels were then reviewed for obvious real-company logos,
public figures, and unsuitable content before deterministic resizing to WebP. No image
generation model or synthetic-media tool was used.
